Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 25, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Brian Santiago LinkedIn n8n Scheduler app. The app is used only by Brian Santiago to schedule and publish LinkedIn posts through a private n8n workflow.

Information Used

The app may use LinkedIn account authorization data needed to publish posts that Brian Santiago approves or schedules. It does not collect passwords.

How Information Is Used

Information is used only to publish scheduled LinkedIn posts from Brian Santiago's own workflow. It is not sold, shared, rented, or used for advertising.

Data Storage

Authorization details may be stored inside the private n8n instance or related local configuration so the workflow can run. Access is limited to the app owner.

Data Sharing

The app does not share personal data with third parties except LinkedIn, as required to publish posts through LinkedIn's API.

Data Removal

Access can be removed by deleting the LinkedIn credential in n8n or revoking the app connection in LinkedIn account settings.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact Brian Santiago through his LinkedIn profile or GitHub profile.
